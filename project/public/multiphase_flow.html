Two-Fluid Euler–Euler Bubble Column

2D air–water column, 0.20 m × 0.50 m, SI units. Both phases have their own momentum equation, coupled through drag and a shared pressure.

Numerics: fractional-step projection on a collocated 40×100 grid, Δt = 4 ms. Implicit drag coupling, donor-cell volume-fraction transport, variable-coefficient pressure Poisson equation. Simplifications: pressure correction is applied to the liquid only (ρg/ρl ≈ 10⁻³), free-slip side walls, open pressure outlet at the top, constant eddy viscosity instead of k–ε, no lift or virtual mass. Bubble size is a transported Sauter-mean field d(x,t): set at the inlet by orifice formation (Tate and Davidson–Schüler), then changed by simple breakup and coalescence closures. This is a single-moment population balance, not a full size distribution.
